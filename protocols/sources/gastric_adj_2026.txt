A Randomised Phase III Trial of Adjuvant Capecitabine Versus Observation in
Resected Stage II Gastric Adenocarcinoma (GASTRIC-ADJ-2026)

PROTOCOL SYNOPSIS

Background and rationale
Gastric cancer incidence in South and East Asia substantially exceeds that of
Western Europe. Adjuvant fluoropyrimidine therapy after D2 gastrectomy has
established benefit, but the regimen studied here was calibrated in largely
European and Japanese cohorts. This trial evaluates the same regimen in a
predominantly South Asian population.

Investigational regimen
Patients randomised to the treatment arm receive capecitabine 1250 mg/m2 orally
twice daily on days 1 through 14 of each 21-day cycle, for a total of eight
cycles. Dose interruptions are permitted for grade 3 or higher toxicity per
institutional standard. Patients randomised to observation receive no adjuvant
systemic therapy.

Inclusion criteria
1. Histologically confirmed gastric or gastro-oesophageal junction
   adenocarcinoma, stage II (AJCC 8th edition).
2. Macroscopically complete D2 gastrectomy within 8 weeks prior to
   randomisation.
3. Age 18 to 75 years inclusive.
4. ECOG performance status 0 or 1.
5. Absolute neutrophil count at least 1.5 x 10^9/L, platelets at least
   100 x 10^9/L, haemoglobin at least 9 g/dL.
6. Serum creatinine clearance at least 50 mL/min by Cockcroft-Gault.
7. Total bilirubin no greater than 1.5 times the upper limit of normal.
8. Written informed consent.

Exclusion criteria
1. Any prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy for gastric malignancy.
2. Known hypersensitivity to capecitabine or other fluoropyrimidines.
3. Uncontrolled cardiac disease, including unstable angina or myocardial
   infarction within 6 months.
4. Active gastrointestinal bleeding or obstruction.
5. Second malignancy within 5 years, excluding adequately treated basal cell
   carcinoma or cervical carcinoma in situ.
6. Pregnancy or lactation; women of childbearing potential must use adequate
   contraception.
7. Any psychiatric or social condition that would preclude protocol compliance.

Participating sites and planned enrolment
- Tata Memorial Centre, Mumbai, India — 140 patients
- Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, Kochi, India — 90 patients
- Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University, Dhaka, Bangladesh — 70 patients
- Charité Universitätsmedizin, Berlin, Germany — 60 patients

Target total enrolment: 360 patients randomised 1:1.

Primary endpoint
Disease-free survival at 3 years.

Secondary endpoints
Overall survival, incidence of grade 3-4 adverse events, treatment
discontinuation rate, quality of life by EORTC QLQ-C30.

Statistical considerations
A sample size of 360 provides 80% power to detect a hazard ratio of 0.70 for
disease-free survival at a two-sided alpha of 0.05, assuming 3 years of accrual
and 2 years of additional follow-up.
