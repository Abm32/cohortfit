ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT01095003
Title: Vinflunine Plus Capecitabine in Advanced Breast Cancer

STUDY DESIGN
Multicenter phase II study of vinflunine in combination with capecitabine in
patients with advanced breast cancer. Target enrolment: 230 subjects.

INVESTIGATIONAL AGENTS
Capecitabine (Xeloda)
  Dose: 1250 mg/m² twice daily
  Route: oral
  Schedule: days 1-14 every 21 days

INCLUSION CRITERIA
- Female, age 21 or older
- Histologically confirmed advanced breast cancer
- Prior anthracycline exposure or taxane resistance
- Adequate bone marrow, hepatic, and renal function per protocol

EXCLUSION CRITERIA
- Known hypersensitivity to capecitabine or fluoropyrimidines
- Severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min)
- Pregnant or breastfeeding
- Uncontrolled intercurrent illness

PLANNED SITES AND ENROLMENT
Mumbai, India — planned enrolment: 100 subjects
Kochi, India — planned enrolment: 50 subjects
Munich, Germany — planned enrolment: 80 subjects

Total planned enrolment (target N): 230
